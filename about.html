About Ditemps

Ditemps is a small India-based project that helps people turn their photos, music and words into a personal surprise page they can share with one link. You do not need to code or design anything: pick a template, add your memories, and send it.

We started Ditemps because most greeting messages feel the same. A message in a chat is read in seconds. A page with your own story, a song they love and a message written for them can be opened again on a hard day.

What we offer

A growing library of templates for birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, friendship days and festivals. Many are free to start; some premium templates have a small price. Every page you create gets a private link and a QR code.

How we work

We design and review every template ourselves, and we add new ones regularly. If something does not work for you, write to us and we will fix it.
