DMCA and Copyright Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026. Ditemps respects the rights of creators and expects its users to do the same.

Reporting infringing content

If you believe something on Ditemps (a template, a photo, music or text) is used without permission, send us a notice at ditempsofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Copyright notice".

What to include

Your name and contact details; a description of the work you own; the link to the page on Ditemps where it appears; a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised; and a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.

What we do

We review each notice promptly and may remove or disable access to the content while we do. We may share your notice with the user who posted the content. Repeat infringers may have their accounts suspended.

Counter-notice

If your content was removed by mistake, email us the link, why you believe the removal was an error, and proof that you have the right to use the material. We will review it and restore the content where appropriate.
