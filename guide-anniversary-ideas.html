Anniversary surprise ideas that cost almost nothing

The best anniversary gifts are rarely the most expensive ones. They work because they show you were paying attention. Here are ideas that cost little and mean a lot.

Build a memory timeline. List the ten moments that shaped your story: the first message, the first trip, the first argument you laughed about later. Add a photo and one line for each. Seeing the story laid out makes people realise how far they have come together.

Write a letter for the future. Write what you love about your partner today and what you hope for the next year, then agree to open it together on the next anniversary. Over the years it becomes a small archive of your relationship.

Recreate the first date. Go to the same place, order the same thing and bring one photo from back then. The effort of remembering the details is the gift.

Finish with something to keep. A shareable page with your photos, a song and a short message can be revisited whenever they miss you, which a one-time dinner cannot do.
