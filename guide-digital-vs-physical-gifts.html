Digital vs physical gifts: when a personalised link wins

Physical gifts have weight and presence, but they can be forgotten in a drawer. Digital surprises are light, instant and easy to revisit, but only work when they are personal.

A personalised link is a good choice when you are far away, when the occasion is last-minute, or when the meaning lives in words, photos and music rather than an object. It is also easy to update or resend on a later date.

A physical gift is better when touch matters, for example jewellery, a book or something handmade. Many people combine both: a small present with a QR code that opens a message and a photo collection.

Whichever you choose, aim for one true detail. An object chosen because of an inside joke, or a page that mentions the exact day you met, feels more valuable than a costly item with no story behind it.
