How to plan a proposal surprise

A proposal does not need a crowd or a viral video. It needs to feel like the two of you. Start by asking what your partner would enjoy: privacy or celebration, a quiet spot or a place with meaning.

Choose the place and time first. Somewhere you have shared a memory works better than somewhere simply scenic. Think about light, weather and how easily you can be alone for a few minutes.

Plan the words, not a script. Write down three things you want to say: what you love about them, one moment you knew, and the question. Practise out loud once so you are not reading from your phone.

Decide who should know. A close friend can help with photos or logistics, and a family member may want to be there later. Keep the circle small so the surprise survives.

Prepare a keepsake. A page that holds your photos, your story and the date lets both of you return to the moment later, and share it with family who could not be there.
