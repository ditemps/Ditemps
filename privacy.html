Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026. This policy explains what information Ditemps collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you sign in we receive basic account details such as your name, email address and profile picture from your sign-in provider. When you create a surprise, we store the photos, text, music links and settings you add so your page can load. We also record basic usage information such as pages viewed and errors, to keep the service working.

How we use it

We use your information to run your account, show your pages to the people you share them with, process purchases and wallet credits, prevent abuse and improve our templates. We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

Ditemps uses Google Firebase for sign-in and data storage. Payments are completed through third-party UPI and payment apps; we receive confirmation of payment but do not ask for your card or bank passwords.

Cookies and advertising

We and our partners may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your settings and measure traffic. If ads are shown, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing ditempsofficial@gmail.com. Shared surprise pages can be deleted from your account at any time.

Children

Ditemps is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
